# GoldBench & RoadTrip

Two independently designed and built apps, shown through their own screens.

**[Open the showcase →](https://zzzly228.github.io)**

## GoldBench — market and macro charts

A chart tool for stocks, ETFs, gold and economic indicators. Pick a symbol and a period and it gives you candles, volume and the numbers for that window. From there you can draw trend lines and levels on the chart, hold two symbols against each other with a ratio chart, or write an expression across several symbols and chart the result as its own candle series.

**[GoldBench →](https://zzzly228.github.io/goldbench.html)**

## RoadTrip — a driving companion for iPhone

Press record and drive: the route is drawn as you go and kept afterwards with its distance and drive time. Photos and videos taken along the way are filed into that day's folder on their own. The map carries the weather above you and the places beside you, including a restaurant's health inspection record, and hands a destination to your navigation app. Saved drives stay on the phone.

**[RoadTrip →](https://zzzly228.github.io/roadtrip.html)**
