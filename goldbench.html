GoldBench RoadTrip

Market and macro charts

Read a market,
then mark it up.

GoldBench charts stocks, ETFs, gold and economic indicators. Pick a symbol and a period and it gives you candles, volume and the numbers that go with them. From there you can draw on the chart, hold two symbols against each other, or write an expression across several of them and chart the result as candles.

Browser and iPhoneCandlestick chartsTwo-symbol comparisonCustom expressionsEconomic indicators

01

One symbol, the whole period

Type a symbol or use a preset. Choose daily, weekly or monthly candles, and a period from three months out to ten years — or a custom start date. The row above the chart holds the last price, the change across the period, its high, its low and its range; volume sits under the candles.

A year of daily gold-ETF candles, priced through early September 2026.

02

Draw on the chart, and keep it

Add a trend line between two points, or a horizontal level that runs across the whole chart. Lines can be edited by date and price, hidden or deleted, and up to five live on a chart at once. They belong to that symbol, interval and period, so they are still there the next time you come back to it.

A trend line under the lows and a level across the period high, both drawn in the app.

03

Two symbols, one time axis

Compare mode takes two symbols and charts their ratio as candles, so relative strength is something you can read rather than infer. Below it, both symbols are redrawn as percentages from a common starting point. Drag one chart and the other follows.

Five years of weekly data for a gold ETF and a broad market ETF.

04

Write the series you want

Type an expression over several symbols and the result comes back as a full candle series, not a closing-price line. The symbols the expression used are listed under the chart and can be opened for their own charts. Drawing works here too.

One expression across two symbols, charted like any other series.

05

The economic backdrop

Yields, inflation expectations, spreads, the policy rate, money supply, unemployment and the dollar index sit next to the market presets and open in the same chart, with the same intervals, periods and drawing tools. They can also be used inside an expression alongside a market symbol.

Ten years of the 10-year Treasury yield, monthly.

How it goes

  1. Pick a symbolType it, or tap one of the presets for a well-known stock, ETF, gold or an economic indicator.
  2. Set the periodDaily, weekly or monthly candles; three months through ten years, or a start date of your own.
  3. Read the numbersLast price, change across the period, high, low, range and volume, all for exactly the window on screen.
  4. Mark it upDraw a trend line or a level. It stays with that chart until you delete it.
  5. Compare or combinePut a second symbol beside it, or write an expression across several and chart the result.
